Document signing

A contract needs two signatures, Legal and Finance, in any order, before a deadline. A skail function sends the contract to both and waits for the signatures or the deadline, whichever comes first. When the contract does not go ahead, it undoes what was done: a signature already given is revoked and a pending request is withdrawn.

You play Legal and Finance:

  1. Click Send contract for signature.
  2. Sign or refuse in each area, in any order, or do nothing and let the deadline (2 minutes) pass.
